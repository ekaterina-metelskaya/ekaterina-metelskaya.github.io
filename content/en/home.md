## How I work {#process label="02 · Approach"}

Every project comes with different goals, constraints, and unknowns. I don’t follow a single process for every situation, but there are a few principles I stick to.

### Understand the domain

Every new product means learning a new domain. I get familiar with its terminology, constraints, and dependencies, and talk to subject-matter experts. I want to understand not only what users do, but why they do it that way.

### Look at the problem from different angles

I start by understanding whose problem we’re solving and what it actually is. I consider user needs, business goals, and technical constraints. I ask questions, clarify requirements, and suggest alternatives when I see a better approach.

### Simplify without losing what matters

Removing elements from a screen doesn’t always make a product simpler. Sometimes the answer is to reorganize information, rethink an interaction, or keep a familiar way of working. I look for solutions that make tasks easier without sacrificing necessary functionality.

### See designs through to release

My work doesn’t end with the mockups. I discuss solutions with developers, account for technical constraints, support implementation, and check how the interfaces work once they’re built.
