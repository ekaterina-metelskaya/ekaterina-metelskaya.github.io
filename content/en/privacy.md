## Who runs this site

metelskaya.design is the personal portfolio of Ekaterina Metelskaya. For questions about your data, email [metelskaya.ek@gmail.com](mailto:metelskaya.ek@gmail.com).

## What data is collected

The site doesn't ask for your name, phone number, or other contact details. Case studies include interactive demo elements. Analytics only runs if you click “Allow” in the consent banner. If you do, Google Analytics 4 and Yandex Metrica collect information about your visit:

- which pages you visit and which website you came from;
- the date and time of your visit, scrolling, section views, interactions with page elements, choices in the “Are we a good fit?” section, and clicks on the CV and contact links;
- your device type, browser, operating system, language, and screen size;
- your approximate location (country and city), based on your IP address;
- browser identifiers stored in Google Analytics cookies (<code>_ga</code>, <code>_ga_QF1XHVLY6B</code>) and Yandex Metrica cookies (such as <code>_ym_uid</code>, <code>_ym_d</code>, <code>_ym_isad</code>).

Yandex Metrica's click map and Session Replay (Webvisor) are enabled. Session Replay records actions on the page, such as pointer movements, clicks, and scrolling, so I can see how visitors use the site. Some of this information may be considered personal data.

Text entered into demo fields in case studies isn't sent through the site's custom analytics events. These fields are also excluded from Webvisor recordings.

## Why this data is collected

To understand which pages and case studies visitors find useful and to improve the site. The data isn't used for advertising: the site does not grant Google consent for advertising cookies or ad personalization.

## Who processes the data

Google and Yandex process the data under their own policies: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/). Their servers may be located outside your country.

## How long data is stored

Data retention periods depend on Google Analytics and Yandex Metrica settings and policies. Aggregated reports may be kept for longer.

## If you don't give consent

Without your permission, Google Analytics and Yandex Metrica aren't loaded and don't collect visit statistics or Webvisor recordings.

Your choice is saved in your browser so you aren't asked on every visit. After Yandex Metrica was added, the site started asking for consent again because the previous choice covered only Google Analytics. The site also remembers your selected color theme separately.

## How to change your choice

Click “Privacy Settings” at the bottom of any page. If you withdraw consent, both analytics services will stop collecting new data. The site will delete the analytics cookies it can access, but it can't delete cookies stored on the services' own domains.

Data collected earlier may still be stored in Google Analytics and Yandex Metrica. If you have questions about how it's processed or deleted, contact me at the email address above.
